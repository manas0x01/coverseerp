    Frequently Asked Questions

    Find answers to common questions about CoverseERP.

    What types of businesses can use CoverseERP?

    Our platform is ideal for startups, SMEs, and enterprises across industries including tech, finance, retail, manufacturing, and service-based businesses. We serve clients across all of India.

    Is employee monitoring software legal and secure?

    Yes, fully compliant when used with employee consent and transparency. All data is securely stored, encrypted, and accessible only to authorized administrators.

    Can I customize modules like Inventory or Accounting?

    Absolutely. Our system is modular and highly configurable to match your workflow, permissions, reporting needs, and business rules.

    Do you offer training or onboarding support?

    Yes. We provide complete onboarding, user manuals, video tutorials, and live training sessions to ensure a smooth start.

    Is CoverseERP cloud-based or on-premise?

    We offer both options. Most clients prefer our secure cloud-hosted solution for easier access and lower IT overhead.

    How does billing work? Monthly or yearly?

    We offer both monthly and annual plans. Contact us at +91 9958089836 or info@coverseerp.in for a custom quote.

    Do you support GST invoicing and Indian accounting standards?

    Yes! CoverseERP is fully GST-compliant, supports HSN/SAC codes, generates GSTR reports, and follows Indian accounting standards.
