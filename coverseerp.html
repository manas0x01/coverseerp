Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up CoverseERP for your multi-company business.

What types of businesses can use CoverseERP?

Our platform is ideal for growing SMEs, manufacturing firms, trading companies, retail chains, contractors, and multi-entity conglomerates. Because our modules can be turned on or off per company, you only use what fits each branch's exact workflow.

How does multi-company management work under one login?

You log in once as master administrator and switch between companies instantly from a top dropdown. Each entity maintains its own segregated books, bank accounts, inventory, and employees, while the master view provides unified rollup financial statements.

Is employee desktop and field monitoring legal and secure?

Yes, CoverseERP is fully compliant with enterprise privacy and data regulations. Workstation tracking only monitors work hours and productive software usage with transparent employee consent. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using bank-grade 256-bit encryption.

Can I customize modules like Inventory or Accounting?

Absolutely. You can customize invoice layouts, chart of accounts, approval workflows, custom tax rules, and user permission groups without needing developer intervention.

Do you provide onboarding and data migration assistance?

Yes. We offer guided CSV migration templates to bring in your existing customers, vendors, opening balances, and employee master records. Our customer success team provides live video walkthroughs and dedicated onboarding support.

Is CoverseERP cloud-hosted or on-premise?

CoverseERP runs on high-availability cloud infrastructure with automated hourly backups. For enterprise groups with specific compliance policies, we also offer dedicated private cloud or on-premise installation options.
