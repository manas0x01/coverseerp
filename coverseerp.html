Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our business software solutions.

What types of businesses can use CoverseERP?

Our platform is ideal for startups, SMEs, and enterprises across industries including manufacturing, retail, tech, construction, logistics, and professional services.

How does multi-company management work under one login?

You log in once as the master administrator and switch between companies from a top dropdown. Each entity maintains its own segregated books, bank accounts, and inventory, while the master view provides unified rollup financial statements.

Is employee monitoring software legal and secure?

Yes, it's fully compliant when used with consent and transparency. Workstation activity tracking focuses on productive hours and software usage. All data is encrypted with bank-grade 256-bit security.

Can I customize modules like Inventory or Accounting?

Absolutely. Our system is modular and configurable to match your workflow, permissions, custom approval matrices, and reporting needs without custom coding.

Do you offer training or onboarding support?

Yes. We provide complete onboarding, guided data migration templates (for customers, vendors, and opening balances), and live training sessions to ensure a smooth transition.

Is this software cloud-based or on-premise?

We offer high-availability cloud hosting by default with automatic backups. We also provide dedicated private cloud or on-premise deployment for enterprise clients.
